About OpenThreadNews

An independent entertainment publication for readers who want more than a recap: background, context, and straight answers. Read the latest at openthreadnews.com.

An independent voice in entertainment

We started OpenThreadNews because entertainment coverage kept getting thinner: shorter posts, hotter takes, less reporting. We wanted a publication that treats pop culture like it matters, because to its fans, it does.

So we built one around a simple rule. Every story gets researched before it gets published. If we cannot verify it, we do not print it. That rule has not changed, and it will not.

Today our flagship coverage follows shows like The Traitors: New Blood, alongside reporting on the influencers, films, and cultural moments driving the conversation. See our Traitors hub for the current focus.

How we work

Research first

Reporting comes before writing. Sources, timelines, and on-the-record detail are the foundation of every piece.

Facts over speed

We would rather publish second and right than first and wrong. Speed is a feature; accuracy is the product.

Corrections, handled openly

When we get something wrong, we fix it and say so. No silent edits, no buried updates. Here is how to reach us if you spot an error.

What "unfiltered" means

It means no PR gloss and no algorithm-chasing. We write for readers, in a direct voice, about what is actually interesting. If a story needs skepticism, it gets skepticism. If it deserves enthusiasm, it gets that too.
