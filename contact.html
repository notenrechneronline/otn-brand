Press & Contact

For press inquiries, corrections, and tips. Everything below goes through the OpenThreadNews editorial team.

Press inquiries

For interview requests, statements, or media partnerships, contact the editorial team through the main site. Include the show or talent involved, your outlet, and your deadline so we can respond in time.

Corrections policy

We fix errors openly and promptly. If you spot something wrong in our coverage, reach the editorial team via openthreadnews.com with a link to the article and a note on what needs fixing. Our standards are set out on the About page.

Tips

The best tips are verifiable and specific: who, what, where, and how we can check it. Send them through openthreadnews.com, and tell us whether you want to stay anonymous.
